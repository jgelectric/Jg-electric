Professional whole-home surge protection

Power surges can come from utility switching, nearby lightning activity, and equipment cycling inside the home. A panel-mounted surge protective device helps reduce surge energy reaching branch circuits.

What this service can include

  • Panel-mounted surge protective devices
  • Compatibility and panel-space evaluation
  • Breaker-connected and integrated options
  • Grounding and bonding review
  • Replacement of failed surge devices
  • Protection planning for sensitive equipment

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Does a whole-home surge protector stop every surge?

No device can prevent every possible event, but a properly installed surge protective device can significantly reduce many common transient overvoltages.

Do I still need plug-in surge strips?

Layered protection can be useful for especially sensitive electronics. Use listed products appropriate for the equipment.

How do I know if the surge protector is working?

Many devices include status indicator lights. The manufacturer instructions explain what the indicators mean and when replacement is needed.
