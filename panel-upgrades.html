Professional electrical panel upgrades

An outdated or undersized panel can limit renovations, EV charging, HVAC upgrades, and other modern electrical loads. JG Electric can evaluate your existing equipment and plan an appropriate upgrade.

What this service can include

  • Main breaker panel replacement
  • 100-amp and 200-amp upgrade planning
  • Subpanel installation
  • Breaker and circuit directory updates
  • Grounding and bonding review
  • Coordination for added loads such as EV chargers

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my panel needs replacement?

Common reasons include obsolete equipment, corrosion, overheating, lack of capacity, damaged components, or a planned increase in electrical load.

Will upgrading the panel increase my electrical capacity?

It can, but capacity depends on the service conductors, utility equipment, meter, grounding, and load calculation—not only the panel itself.

Do panel upgrades require permits?

Panel and service upgrades generally require permits and inspections. Requirements vary by municipality and project scope.
