Modern kitchen lighting upgrade

Flexible lighting, hidden power, and thoughtful details that improve both the appearance and everyday function of the kitchen.

Request a Similar ProjectCall 603-233-0736
The homeowner's goal

A brighter kitchen without visual clutter.

The goal was to create comfortable, even lighting for daily use while preserving the clean appearance of the new backsplash and cabinetry. The homeowner also wanted softer illumination at night and convenient task lighting beneath the cabinets.

Project TypeKitchen Remodel
ServicesLighting & Power
RegionSouthern New Hampshire

Our solution

  • Recessed LED lighting with integrated night-light mode
  • Motion-activated under-cabinet lighting
  • Hidden under-cabinet power outlets
  • Clean device placement and finish details

The result

The completed installation provides strong working light when needed, warm low-level illumination at night, and convenient countertop power without interrupting the backsplash design.
