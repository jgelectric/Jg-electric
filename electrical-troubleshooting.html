Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

A breaker may trip because of an overload, short circuit, ground fault, damaged equipment, or a wiring problem. Testing is needed to identify the actual cause.

Can you trace a circuit that is not labeled?

Yes. Circuit tracing can help identify which breaker supplies an outlet, light, appliance, or other load.

Should I reset a breaker repeatedly?

No. A breaker that continues to trip is warning of a problem. Avoid repeated resets and schedule an inspection.
