Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a dedicated circuit?

It is a circuit intended to supply a specific appliance or piece of equipment rather than several unrelated loads.

Will a dedicated circuit stop my breaker from tripping?

It can resolve overload-related tripping when the existing circuit is serving too many loads, but the cause should be diagnosed first.

What wire and breaker size will be used?

Sizing depends on the equipment rating, circuit length, installation method, and applicable electrical requirements.
