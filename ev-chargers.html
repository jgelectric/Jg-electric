Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What size circuit does an EV charger need?

It depends on the charger output and manufacturer instructions. Common installations use 40-, 50-, or 60-amp circuits, but the correct size must be verified.

Can my existing panel support an EV charger?

A load calculation and panel evaluation are needed. In some cases, load management or a panel upgrade may be appropriate.

Is hardwiring better than using a receptacle?

Hardwiring can reduce connection points and may support higher charging rates. The best option depends on the charger, location, and your preferences.
