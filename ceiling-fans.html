Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a fan use the same box as a light fixture?

Only if the existing box is specifically listed and supported for fan use. Standard light fixture boxes may not be adequate.

Can the fan and light be controlled separately?

Yes, when the wiring and fan controls support separate operation.

Do you assemble the fan?

Fan installation typically includes reasonable assembly, mounting, wiring, and basic operation testing.
